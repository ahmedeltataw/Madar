    Platform Features

    Everything you need to manage your trips

    Core tools

    Features designed to save your time

    Everything you need to manage your trips and follow up with participants, from data collection to ready-to-use reports.

    Automatic attendance & apology confirmation

    Automatic messages confirm participants' attendance or receive their apologies without manual follow-up.

    Participant data collection

    Custom registration forms for each trip that collect participant data automatically and in an organized way.

    Post-trip evaluations

    Receive participant evaluations after each trip to keep improving your experience.

    Excel data export

    Export participant data and reports to Excel in one click, ready for analysis.

    Duplicate registration prevention

    The platform prevents the same participant from registering in more than one trip within the same group.

    Per-trip settings

    Full control of the registration form and the required data for each trip.

    Comprehensive search & filter

    Search and filter participants quickly by status or any information you need.

    Detailed analytics

    A live analytics dashboard showing your trips and participant metrics in real time.
