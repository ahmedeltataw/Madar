      Subscription requirements

      What do you need to subscribe?

      A few simple requirements ensure a reliable experience for your organization — gather them and start your journey with us.

      1. 1 Step one

        Valid official license

        A valid license or commercial record proving your organization's activity.

      2. 2 Step two

        Supervisor delegation

        Appoint a person responsible for managing the account, with their official details.

      3. 3 Step three

        Responsibility statement

        A signed statement accepting responsibility for managing trips and data.

      4. 4 Step four

        Subscription payment

        Choose the right plan and complete your payment securely.

      5. 5 Step five

        Upload organization logo

        Upload your company or association logo to appear on tickets and reports.

      The delegation form is printed on official letterhead matching the license or commercial record.
